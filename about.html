About us

35+ years in the bushveld

Our story

Bonwa Phala is a private game lodge on 5,000 hectares of bushveld outside Bela-Bela, less than two hours’ drive from Johannesburg and Pretoria.

Who we are

For more than 35 years we’ve welcomed families, groups of friends, hunters, photographers and wedding parties to the reserve. More than 50 animal species live here, and the bush is never far from your door.

Guests stay in one of two lodges: the thatched Main Lodge with its ten chalets, or the tented Bush Lodge. Book a single room, or take a whole lodge for a wedding, conference or celebration.

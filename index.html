More than a stay

  • Game drives

    On the reserve

    More than 50 animal species live on the 5,000-hectare reserve. Head out with a guide in the early morning or late afternoon.

  • Photographic safaris

    On the reserve

    Smaller groups, more guides per photographer and comfortable lodging between shoots, built around the best light.

  • Hunting packages

    On the reserve

    Plains game and buffalo packages with professional, well-trained guides and comfortable hunting accommodation.

  • Weddings

    Events

    Say your vows beneath the Rock Fig or a majestic Marula tree, then dine in the bush. Small, affordable and entirely yours.

  • Conferences & retreats

    Events

    A fully equipped conference centre at the Main Lodge, with the reserve on hand for breakaways and team time.

  • Celebrations

    Events

    Birthdays, bachelor and bachelorette weekends, family reunions and corporate outings, with the lodge to yourselves.

  • Golf, spa & day trips

    Nearby

    Zebula Golf Estate & Spa is about 10 minutes away. Fercare Wildlife Centre and Adventures with Elephants are close by too.
